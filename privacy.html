Privacy Policy

ClipsUploader is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own accounts (YouTube channel, Instagram account, Facebook page). It is not offered to other users.

  • The app accesses Google/YouTube, Instagram and Facebook data only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own accounts.
  • Access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the respective account settings (e.g. myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Data deletion: All locally stored tokens can be deleted by removing the app's configuration file; access can also be revoked in the account settings of each platform.

Datenschutzerklärung

ClipsUploader wird ausschließlich vom Betreiber genutzt, um Videos auf seinen eigenen Konten zu veröffentlichen. Zugriffsschlüssel werden nur lokal auf dem eigenen Rechner gespeichert. Es werden keine Daten verkauft, an Dritte weitergegeben oder für Werbung genutzt. Der Zugriff kann jederzeit in den Kontoeinstellungen der jeweiligen Plattform widerrufen werden.
